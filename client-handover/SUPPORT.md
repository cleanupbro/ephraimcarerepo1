## What's Included (30-Day Support)

For 30 days after handover (until **18 March 2026**), the following is included at no extra cost:

- Fixing any bugs or broken pages
- Fixing form submission issues
- Fixing notification delivery problems
- Minor text changes (typos, phone number updates, etc.)

## How To Request Changes

1. **Email** theopbros.ai@gmail.com
2. Describe what you need in plain English (e.g., "Change the phone number to 0400 123 456")
3. Your developer will make the change, usually within 24-48 hours

---

## What You Can't Change Yourself

This is a **public marketing website** — there is no login or admin panel for it. All changes go through your developer. This keeps the website secure and prevents accidental breakage.

Things that require your developer:
- Changing text, images, or content on any page
- Adding new pages
- Updating contact details
- Adding or removing services
- Making design changes

---

## After The 30-Day Period

After the free support period, changes and updates can be arranged on a per-request basis. Contact your developer to discuss pricing for ongoing maintenance or new features.
