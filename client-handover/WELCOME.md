## What Was Built

A professional public website for **Ephraim Care** — your NDIS disability and mental health support business. The website is designed to bring in new participants and referrals through Google search.

This is a **marketing website**. It does not have any login, password, or admin area. Visitors land on the site, learn about your services, and contact you through forms.

| Page | What It Does |

## How You Get New Clients

1. Someone searches Google for "NDIS provider Prestons" or "disability support near me"
2. Your website appears in the results
3. They visit your site and fill out the Contact or Referral form
4. You get an instant notification on your phone
5. You follow up and onboard the participant

That's it. The website works for you 24/7.
