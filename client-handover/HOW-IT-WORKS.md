# How Your Website Works

A simple guide to how your website brings in new participants and referrals.

---

## The Forms

Your website has three forms that visitors can fill out:

### 1. Contact Form (ephraimcare.com.au/contact)
- Visitors type their name, email, phone, and message
- When they press "Send", the information is saved automatically
- You get a notification on your phone straight away

### 2. Referral Form (ephraimcare.com.au/referrals)
- Support coordinators, LACs, or participants submit a referral
- They fill in the participant's details, NDIS number, and support needs
- When they press "Submit", the referral is saved automatically
- You get a notification on your phone straight away

### 3. Complaints Form (ephraimcare.com.au/complaints)
- Anyone can submit feedback or a complaint
- The submission is saved automatically

---

## Notifications

When someone fills out a form on your website, you get notified instantly via:
- **Telegram** (message to your phone)

This means you never miss a new enquiry or referral — even at 2am.

> **Note:** For notifications to work, your Telegram Bot Token and Chat ID must be set up in Vercel. Your developer has the details if this needs to be configured.

## Google & SEO (Search Engine Optimisation)

Your website has been set up to rank well on Google for searches like:
- "NDIS provider Prestons"
- "NDIS provider near me"
- "disability support Liverpool NSW"
- "NDIS services Western Sydney"

### What was done:
- Every page has search-friendly titles and descriptions
- A dedicated **Prestons location page** targets local searches
- Structured data (special code that Google reads) tells Google your business name, address, phone, and services
- A sitemap is set up so Google can find all your pages easily

### What to expect:
- It takes **2-4 weeks** for Google to fully index new pages
- Rankings improve over time as Google sees your site is active and trustworthy
- You don't need to do anything — the SEO works automatically

## The Prestons Page

The page at **ephraimcare.com.au/prestons** is a special landing page for your Prestons office location. It:
- Targets people searching for "NDIS provider Prestons 2170"
- Shows your office address, phone, services, and nearby suburbs you cover
- Includes FAQs specific to Prestons
- Helps you rank #1 on Google for local Prestons searches
